Roadmap to the Platinum

Reported difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx), roughly 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies, no difficulty settings, no mission replay.

  1. Step 1 — Story & side missions (60–80 h). Play the story and pick up optional missions as they appear. Do the Chapter 1 meditation moment at Jason's house the first time Lucia mentions it (see the missable warning below). Scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app and study animals through binoculars as you go — both feed the 100% checklist. Keep a rotating save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots are reported).
  2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist & Challenges (~100 h). After the ending, the checklist appears under Progress > Stats. It reportedly wants every robbery, underwater discovery, shooting range, drone race and Bret quest, 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals — but most side quests are not required. Only 70 of 80 Challenges are needed for 100%, while the trophy itself needs just 50.
  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup (~10 h). The checklist ignores the stranger questlines, so they are usually what remains, together with stray stunts, scans and card sets. Never enable cheats on your platinum save: they reportedly disable trophies and saving.
⚠ Only reported missable: Positive Mental Attitude. The first meditation, with Lucia on the balcony of Jason's house in Chapter 1, cannot be repeated after Chapter 1 and may count toward the 18 spots. Do it the moment it is offered.
